About

Lunix

Lunix is a small, independent software development company founded by Simeon Dijksma in 2026. We build custom websites, web applications, and dashboards for small businesses, startups, and anyone with an idea worth building.

Every Lunix project is built directly with the client, not simply for them. We believe your software should reflect what you want, rather than being forced into a predetermined corporate style. From the design and user experience to the underlying code, every decision is made with intention.

We place particular emphasis on accessibility, design, security, performance, and maintainable code. We don't believe software should be more complicated than it needs to be, nor do we believe quality should be sacrificed for convenience.

Lunix is also deliberately 100% human-made. No AI is used to build our software, and we have no interest in creating AI-powered products. We believe there is value in knowing that a real person designed, wrote, and stands behind the software you're using.
